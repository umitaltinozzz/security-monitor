# Explain finding urgency

1. Read the recorded severity, priority score and reasons, exploit signals, affected component, and deployment context.
2. Separate product-calculated priority from additional context that may affect response order.
3. Identify missing or stale inputs and lower confidence when evidence is incomplete.
4. Recommend a review order with a concise evidence-based reason.
5. Do not change priority, finding status, risk acceptance, reachability, or false-positive decisions.
